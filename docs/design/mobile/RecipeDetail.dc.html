Lemon Herb Salmon with Quinoa

Oven-roasted salmon with a lemon, garlic and parsley oil, on fluffy quinoa with blistered tomatoes and spinach.

Ingredients

2
300 gsalmon fillet
150 gquinoa
200 gcherry tomatoes
100 gbaby spinach
2 tbspolive oil
1lemon
2 clovesgarlic
15 gfresh parsley

Instructions

  1. 1Rinse the quinoa and simmer it in 300 ml salted water for 15 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  2. 2Mix the olive oil with the lemon zest, crushed garlic and half the parsley.
  3. 3Rub the salmon fillet with the olive oil mix and roast at 200°C for 12 minutes.
  4. 4Halve the cherry tomatoes and wilt them with the spinach in a hot pan for 2 minutes.
  5. 5Toss the quinoa with the tomatoes, spinach and the lemon juice.
  6. 6Serve the salmon on the quinoa and finish with the rest of the parsley.
