Lemon Herb Salmon with Quinoa

Pan-seared salmon over lemony quinoa with blistered cherry tomatoes and a fresh dill yogurt sauce.

Contains Dairy. This recipe conflicts with your allergies or diet — check the ingredients or swap it.

Ingredients

2
  • 300 gsalmon fillets
  • 150 gquinoa
  • 200 gcherry tomatoes
  • 120 gGreek yogurt
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1lemon
  • 10 gfresh dill

Instructions

  1. 1Rinse the quinoa under cold water until the water runs clear.
  2. 2Add the quinoa to a pot with 300 ml water and a pinch of salt, and bring to a boil.
  3. 3Cover, lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes, until the water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork.
  4. 4Meanwhile, pat the salmon dry and season it with salt, pepper and lemon zest.
  5. 5Sear the salmon skin-side down in the olive oil for 4 minutes, flip and cook 3 minutes more.
  6. 6Blister the cherry tomatoes with the garlic in the same pan for 2 minutes.
  7. 7Stir the dill and lemon juice into the yogurt, then serve the salmon over the quinoa with the sauce.
