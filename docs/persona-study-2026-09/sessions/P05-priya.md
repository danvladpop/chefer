# P05 — Priya, 34, London software engineer, lacto-vegetarian (no eggs), wants 15-minute weeknight cooking from her saved recipes

L1 · iPhone 16e (iOS 26, 390×844 pt) · tier at start FREE → end PREMIUM (upgraded in S1, trigger: the Import Recipe lock card — import is the reason she installed; researcher briefly downgraded/re-upgraded in the sweep) · build f8f7f74 · 2026-09-27

## 0. Expectations before opening

- She has ~200 saved recipe reels on Instagram and a Notion page of links she never cooks. She downloaded Chefer hoping it turns "saved" into "cooked": pull those recipes in, and put four of them on weeknights.
- She expects to say "vegetarian, no eggs" once and never see meat, fish or egg again, including sneaky ones (egg noodles, mayo, Worcestershire, gelatine, parmesan with rennet she would tolerate).
- She expects import from a URL and pasted text to "just work", and hopes an Instagram/YouTube link does too.
- Weeknight dinners must be ≤ 15–20 minutes, for two people. UK context: metric, GBP.
- Deal-breakers in the first 5 minutes: weight-loss/diet-culture framing front and centre (calorie shaming, "cheat meals"), a meat recipe after saying vegetarian, or import that only works behind a paywall with no warning.

## 4. Researcher sweep

**Where the missing/unfound things actually are**

- **Free recipe import** — does not exist on mobile: Import is premium-only (the API comment mentions a free "ghost state"/1 preview a day and a canned example, but on this build the free user sees only the lock card, screenshots/P05/014-import-sheet.png, 134-sw-free-import-after-downgrade.png). The only free path is the manual Create Recipe form.
- **Paste-to-parse with review for link/text imports** — exists only for Video (editable draft with "please check" flags). Link/text imports go straight from a two-card summary to save; editing happens afterwards via the card pencil.
- **Photo import** — supported by the API, not offered in the mobile Import tabs (M52).
- **"No eggs"** — the working path is Preferences → Food safety → **Allergies** "eggs". "No eggs" as a dietary restriction is silently ignored (M48). There is no egg-free / lacto-vegetarian option in any list.
- **Time-based planning ("≤ 15 min dinners")** — does not exist. No time preference, no dinners-only or nights selection, no time filter in Replace meal; Discover's only time chip is "≤ 30 min" (M36, M56).
- **"What can I make from my fridge"** — exists as AI Chef + Shop → "In my kitchen" (pantry), but the chat doesn't use ingredients typed in the message, the pantry is named differently in each place ("pantry" in chat, "In my kitchen" in Shop), and matches aren't safety-filtered (M37, M38, M49).
- **Folders / collections / tags for recipes** — do not exist; only a heart ("Saved") (M42).
- **Source link on imported recipes** — stored (`sourceUrl` in DB) but not shown anywhere on the recipe page (M20).
- **Edit from recipe detail** — not there; edit is the small pencil on "Mine" cards only (M44).
- **Default servings = 2 / household of two** — More → Household exists ("Just you at the table for now", "+ My partner", portions ½–1½, per-person diet/allergies; safety free, scaling premium). Priya never looked there because nothing in onboarding's "Eat better" path or the plan pointed to it (screenshots/P05/127-sw-household.png).
- **Currency** — Preferences → Units & currency (EUR/USD/GBP/RON). RON was seeded from the simulator's en_RO region, so it's an environment artifact; a UK phone would get GBP (screenshots/P05/124-sw-prefs-2.png).

**Additional expert findings** (logged as sweep rows M48–M57)

- Three root causes explain the persona's worst moments, all confirmed read-only in source: (1) unknown free-text restriction → literal word match (`apps/api/src/lib/curated-recipes/safety.ts`, `isRecipeSafe`), (2) pantry chat tool has no safety filter (`apps/api/src/application/pantry/pantry.service.ts`, `whatCanIMake`), (3) mobile edit form hard-codes `dietaryTags: []` (`apps/mobile/app/recipe-form.tsx`).
- The Cookbook "All" tab = recipes referenced in meal plans only (`favourite-recipe.repository.ts` `findAllRecipesForUser`), which is why imports are missing from "All" until placed in a plan (M18).
- Premium quota copy: "Recipe imports 0/5" was still 0 immediately after an import and 3/5 later; 5 imports/day is the premium cap.
- Downgrade ("Switch back to the free plan") is instant, keeps data, and the usage card collapses to "Meal plans generated 2/3" (screenshots/P05/131-sw-downgrade.png). Premium sheet re-appears on re-upgrade with a different order (M51).
- The Profile "Your data" card offers Export my data / Delete account — good hygiene (screenshots/P05/131-sw-downgrade.png, first capture).

## 6. Researcher summary (out of character)

- **Diet safety breaks for a very common diet and destroys trust (Sev 4, three independent causes).** "No eggs" as a free-text restriction is silently a no-op (eggs in 4 plan slots, no badges); the AI Chef's kitchen matcher suggests egg and turkey dishes because it skips the safety filter; and editing any imported recipe strips its "vegetarian" tag, producing a false "Contains non-vegetarian" warning on detail and in cook mode. A structured diet picker (incl. lacto-vegetarian / egg-free) and one shared safety filter on every suggestion surface would remove all three.
- **The app is built around a 7-day, 4-meal, kcal-target plan; Priya's job is "4 quick dinners for 2 from recipes I already love".** There is no dinners-only / nights / time / servings input anywhere (onboarding, Preferences, Generate, Replace), no ≤15-min filter, and Regenerate silently discards user-chosen dinners. Time is this persona's primary constraint and the product doesn't model it.
- **Import is the acquisition hook for "saved-recipe" users and it's hard-paywalled at first touch**, with no preview or free taste, and the See Premium path lands on a Profile card that doesn't mention import or price. She upgraded only because "free for now" cost nothing; with a real price this is likely the uninstall moment.
- **The video import draft is the best pattern in the app** (editable, flagged uncertainties, "what we guessed", privacy line). Link and text imports lack it: no ingredient review before save, a calorie-discrepancy banner first, and a pre-selected "Cheferized" variant that rescales to 1 serving and ignores "serves 2".
- **Imported recipes are hard to find again:** not in "All" (All = plan recipes only), save lands on Profile with no confirmation, no source link, search state bleeds across tabs into a wrong "use the web app" empty state, and organisation is a single heart.
- **Diet-culture framing leaks into a user who opted out:** after skipping the goal she still gets a 2,000 kcal ring as the home screen, "Complete your profile (body metrics)" on re-entry, a post-upgrade sheet whose primary CTA is "Set your goal & body metrics", calorie-first import banners, and a calorie-themed chat example. For a persona sceptical of diet apps this undermines the "cook what you save" promise.
- **What works and should be protected:** "YOUR RECIPES" first in Replace meal, fast import turnaround, honest AI consent sheet, servings stepper, cook mode, the "In my kitchen" explanation, and the free-plan chat lock that points to free alternatives.
- **Re-entry has no hook for tonight:** Today at dinner-time shows "Next meal: breakfast", a weight box and "Start training"; nothing like "Tonight: 15-min paneer curry — you have the spinach".
