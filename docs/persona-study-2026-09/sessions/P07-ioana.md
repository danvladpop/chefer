# P07 — Ioana, 27, PhD student and amateur powerlifter cutting 66 → 61 kg, logs every bite (Iași)

L4 · Pixel 8 emulator (Android, 1080×2400, dark mode) · tier at start FREE → end PREMIUM (upgraded in S1, trigger: "your personal nutrition profile" in the Go Premium pitch, hoping for custom targets) · build f8f7f74 · 2026-09-27

## 0. Expectations before opening

- She has a powerlifting meet in 10 weeks and is cutting from 66 to 61 kg on 2,000 kcal / 150 g protein. She weighs everything at home and logs it in MacroFactor (Cronometer before that).
- The weak spot in her logging is eating out with friends twice a week: she guesses, and she hates guessing. She downloaded Chefer to see whether "snap to log" can give her a restaurant meal's macros from a photo, with numbers she can believe and correct.
- She expects to type her own targets (2,000 kcal, 150 g protein) and have the app respect them exactly, not "recommend" something else.
- She expects logging a known food in ≤ 3 taps, grams as the default unit, and a "remaining" number for the day that's always one glance away.
- Deal-breakers in the first 5 minutes: can't set her own targets, logging needs more taps than MacroFactor, numbers that are obviously wrong (e.g. 100 g chicken breast at 300 kcal), or a photo estimate she can't edit. Dark mode that flashes white would annoy her all day.

## 4. Researcher sweep

**Where the missing / unfound things actually are**

- **Custom calorie / macro targets** — do not exist. Targets always come from `resolveDailyTargets` (`apps/api/src/application/preferences/preferences.service.ts`): Mifflin-style estimate from weight/height/age/activity/goal, −500 for Lose Weight, floor 1,200, plus the coach's `targetAdjustmentKcal` dial; protein comes from the goal split. The `dailyCalorieTarget` column is only a fallback when body metrics are missing. Mobile Preferences shows the number read-only (premium adds "… kcal / day — current"); web Preferences is also read-only. The premium row "Full setup: cuisine, cadence & targets" contains no target step. For a user with a coach-set target this is the single biggest blocker (M05, M06).
- **Food search / database / barcode / recents / copy day** — do not exist. Off-plan logging is Quick add (free text + kcal + optional macros) or Snap to log. The fast path that does exist is ticking **planned** meals with portion chips on the Tracker, which Ioana never saw because she had no plan (M12, M40).
- **Edit a logged food entry** — does not exist for Quick add / scan entries (only a delete bin, `deleteCustom` with no confirm). Weight entries do have edit ✎ (Progress) (M24, M25).
- **Adjust a snap estimate** — not possible: the confirm card has meal chips, Discard and Log only (`scan-meal-card.tsx`). The meal slot defaults to Lunch regardless of time (`useState('lunch')`), Quick add defaults to Snack (M20, M27, M28).
- **Snap to log on free** — hidden, not locked: `ScanMealCard` returns `null` when the tier has zero scans ("the profile page carries the upsell"), but the free Go Premium pitch doesn't mention photo logging. Premium: 10 scans/day (M11).
- **Weekly adherence** — no view for a logger without a plan. Progress has 28-day calorie/macro charts and "Days logged / Avg kcal / vs target" tiles, which would cover it once the date bug is fixed; there's no "days within ±x%" metric. The premium weekly coach review (`ChefReviewBanner`) only appears once a weekly review exists — untestable in one calendar day (M34).
- **Goal weight / weight trend** — Progress weight chart plots entries only; no goal weight, moving average or weekly rate. The onboarding weight (66.4 kg) is stored on the profile, not as a weigh-in, so the chart starts with one point (M33).
- **Week rebalance** — `RebalanceBanner` is wired into the Tracker for premium logs; it did not appear after going 73–468 kcal over the computed target in this session. Not verified whether it needs a plan for the rest of the week.

**Additional expert findings** (sweep rows M35–M41): local-only plan ticks behind "Save Day" (M35), inconsistent over-target colours (M36), camera-denied dead end (M37), no Quick add validation (M38), generated plan misses its own targets (M39, mock), planned-meal ticking with portion chips (M40, delight), light app between dark system sheets (M41).

**Bugs & LogBox errors**

- No red error boxes or yellow LogBox warnings were seen.
- **M32 — Progress shows "Days logged 0 / No log data yet" right after midnight local time.** Root cause (code read): `trackerService.summary()` builds the day list with `new Date()` + `setUTCDate`/`toISOString()` — i.e. UTC days. At 02:00 EEST (23:00 UTC on the 26th) the 28-day window ends on 26 Sep, so the 27 Sep log (row verified read-only in `daily_logs`, date 2026-09-27, 5 entries) is outside it. Affects every user east of UTC for 2–3 hours after local midnight, and users west of UTC in the evening in the opposite direction. Repro: log food at 00:00–03:00 local in Romania, open More → Progress.
- **M07 — imperial + USD default for a Romanian user.** `auth.service.ts` applies "location defaults" from a client-sent region; the emulator's device locale is en-US, so she got Imperial/USD despite typing cm/kg in onboarding. Region-from-language is a poor proxy (many Europeans run their phone in English); onboarding measurements typed in metric could override it.
- **M21 — Today lagged the Tracker by ~8 s** after logging a scan (1,122 → 1,552). Host was heavily loaded; may just be refetch latency.
- **M15** — 6.5 g fat is stored as 6.5 (DB) but displayed as 7 g.
- Environment only (not product): System UI ANR at first launch; two blank reloads during typing and 2-minute blank cold starts (host load ~20–33); frequent Metro "Refreshing…" banners.

## 6. Researcher summary (out of character)

- **Precision trackers are blocked on step one: targets can't be set manually.** Calories and macros are always computed (1,669 kcal / 146 g P for her) and shown read-only everywhere; even the premium "Full setup: … & targets" has no target step. Every downstream screen (ring, "73 over", orange bars, plan day totals) then judges her against a number she rejects. A simple override (kcal + protein, optionally carbs/fat) would unblock this whole segment (M05, M06, M29).
- **Off-plan logging is "bring your own numbers".** No food search, database, barcode, recents or copy-day; Quick add is framed as "off-plan… log it honestly… roughly". For someone who logs every bite, that is ~50 s and 9 taps per item from memory (M12, M13, M14).
- **Logged entries can't be edited; delete is instant with no undo.** Correcting a mistake means delete + re-type everything (M24, M25).
- **Snap-to-log is the hook and it's fast and trustworthy in framing, but not correctable.** Consent sheet, system photo picker and the ~3 s estimate with a stated portion assumption are genuinely good (M18, M19, M26). But no portion/macro/item adjustment, no photo thumbnail, unexplained "CONFIDENT", and a wrong dish can only be discarded (M20, M27). A "bigger / smaller / not this" correction step would turn it into a MacroFactor-beating feature.
- **The feature she installed for was invisible on free.** The Snap card is not rendered at all for free users and the Premium pitch doesn't mention photo logging; she found it only because she upgraded for a different reason (M10, M11).
- **Progress says "0 days logged" after local midnight because the summary uses UTC days** — a real bug that makes the week/adherence view look broken for every user in Romania from 00:00 to ~03:00 (M32). Beyond the bug, there's no weekly adherence metric or weight trend/goal line (M33, M34).
- **Mixed save model on the Tracker:** quick adds and scans save instantly, planned-meal ticks only on "Save Day", yet the bars update immediately in both cases. Users who never tap Save Day lose ticks (M17, M35).
- **Defaults fight the user:** Imperial/USD for a Romanian who just typed cm/kg (from an en-US phone language), meal slot "Lunch" for a 2 am scan, protein-over-target in warning orange on a cut (M07, M28, M29).
