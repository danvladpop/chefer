## 0. Expectations before opening

- His daughter Sophie installed Chefer on his phone after his GP appointment: HbA1c borderline ("pre-diabetes"), lose about 10 kg (102 kg → ~92 kg, 180 cm), and try strength training twice a week.
- He expects the app to ask about his blood sugar and give him simple advice: what to eat today, what to cut back on (bread? potatoes? sugar?), and why. He wants plain English, big text, and nothing he can break.
- His wife Karen cooks most meals; he wants to "do his part", not become a chef. He's never been in a gym and expects something gentle that tells him exactly what to do.
- He'd delete it in the first 5 minutes if: the text is too small to read even with his glasses, it's full of gym jargon or numbers he doesn't understand, it asks for a card, or he feels he's pressed something he can't undo.
- Would pay if his GP would approve of it — i.e. if it looks medically sensible.

## 4. Researcher sweep

**Where the missing / unfound things actually are**

- **A place to tell the app about pre-diabetes / blood sugar** — does not exist. No health-condition step in onboarding or Preferences; diet options are Omnivore/Vegetarian/Vegan/Pescatarian/Keto/Paleo/Gluten-Free/Dairy-Free (`apps/mobile/src/features/preferences/types.ts`). A free-text restriction like "pre-diabetes" falls through `RESTRICTION_RULES` and is treated as a literal word to avoid in recipe text (`apps/api/src/lib/curated-recipes/safety.ts` ~L170–242), so it changes nothing. The string _is_ forwarded to the AI chat/plan context (seen in the mock echo, 054).
- **"Why" for meals and targets** — there's no per-meal rationale anywhere. The only explanation of a number is Preferences → Goal & body ("2,099 kcal · 2,599 maintenance · 203 g protein — 2.0 g/kg because you train"), which Tom only found on day 2 while looking for currency.
- **Target weight / "10 kg to go"** — does not exist (no goal-weight field in `ChefProfile`; Progress only shows first-vs-latest weight delta).
- **Medical disclaimer** — none in the app. The only guardrail text is in the server-side weekly coach-review prompt ("You are a chef, not a doctor…", `apps/api/src/lib/ai/prompts.ts`), never shown to users; the chat prompt has no health guardrail.
- **Log a meal without knowing calories** — exists, but not in Quick add: Snap to log (photo estimate, on Today and Tracker) and AI Chef (premium) estimate calories. Quick add requires a number. Tom never considered Snap to log for a sandwich he'd already eaten.
- **Why the chat-logged lunch vanished** — `chat.service.ts` logs to `new Date().toISOString()` (UTC date) while Today/Tracker use the device's local date; at 00:55 local (UTC+3) the entry landed on 26 Sep. A UK user hits this 00:00–01:00 BST; the study's RO-region device made it 00:00–03:00. The mock always files chat logs as "snack" (the tool schema also defaults to snack).
- **Currency** — seeded from device region at sign-up (RO → RON), changeable in More → Preferences → Units & currency (works, "Saved ✓"; plan and shop switched to £ immediately, 124/125). Not asked or shown in onboarding.
- **Changing training days** — Gym → Routine → Edit → "Planned weekday" chips (worked). Reminder time / weekly goal: only in `/gym/settings`, effectively unreachable (no gear icon; only an offline-sync banner links there). Equipment access (Full gym vs Dumbbells vs Bodyweight) cannot be changed after the wizard.
- **Where the targets moved** — protein switches from the 35/35/30 split (184 g) to 2.0 g/kg of latest weigh-in once gym setup is complete (204 g → 203 g after the 101.6 kg weigh-in); carbs absorb the difference. Silent to the user.

**Additional expert findings** (logged as `sweep` rows M39–M47): no medical framing anywhere (M39), the plan contradicting its own protein target and telling a dieter to eat more (M40), unreachable gym settings / fixed equipment (M41), workout logger at XXL text (M42 delight), delete-account and forgot-password flows (M43 delight), Progress UTC/cache mismatch (M44), two sources of body weight (M45), feedback box (M46 delight), sign-out without confirmation (M47).

Problems seen: Weekly outlook clips the last day (025/055), week-summary sheet breaks day labels mid-word (043), Edit routine truncates exercise names to "Dumbbe…" ×3 and clips "Add exercis" (111), Profile household line truncated (037), ring label crowds the ring (025), long pages (Preferences ≈ 6 screens).
