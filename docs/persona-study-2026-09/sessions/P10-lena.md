## 0. Expectations before opening

- She has been looking for a meal planner that does **vegan and strictly gluten-free (coeliac)** at the same time _and_ knows she trains (long runs need carbs; two lifting days need protein). Cronometer is accurate but doesn't plan; Yazio's plans kept suggesting seitan and couscous.
- She expects to state "vegan + coeliac" once and never see honey, eggs, dairy, wheat, barley, rye, seitan, couscous, spelt or regular oats again. Cross-contamination matters (oats must be certified GF).
- As a UX researcher and GDPR-aware person, she will read every consent prompt, decline analytics/tracking, and look for who the AI provider is and what gets sent.
- She wants numbers she can trace: "where does 2,400 kcal come from?", "is this per serving?".
- Deleting in the first 5 minutes: a dark-pattern consent screen (pre-ticked tracking, no "decline"), or one meal in her plan with gluten or an animal product after she set her diet.

## 4. Researcher sweep

**Where the missing/unfound things actually are**

- **Analytics/tracking consent** — there is nothing to decline. The mobile app ships no analytics SDK (`apps/mobile/src/features/gym/analytics.ts` is an explicit no-op). `EXPO_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN` is declared but no Sentry package is installed, and there is no ATT prompt. The API runs Sentry with `tracesSampleRate: 1` (`apps/api/src/instrument.ts`). Whether the privacy policy discloses this could not be checked, because web pages aren't served in this setup.
- **"Where does the number come from"** — the protein rule exists: Preferences → Goal & body, "93 g protein / day — Protein set from your bodyweight (1.6 g/kg) because you train." The kcal target has no formula or source anywhere in the UI. Nothing on Today or Plan links to the explanation (M36, M37).
- **Training-aware food** — does not exist for her.
  - Only GAIN_MUSCLE lifters get per-day training variation (`packages/utils/src/training-nutrition.ts`, `hasTrainingDayBump`).
  - MAINTAIN gets one flat target, and running/cardio is not modelled anywhere.
  - Finishing the gym wizard _does_ change food macros (lifter protein), but silently (M50, M51).
- **Coeliac / cross-contamination** — no such option; the diet is free text only.
  - Safety uses a regex matcher (`apps/api/src/lib/curated-recipes/safety.ts`). "gluten" expands to a list that includes oats (good for coeliac).
  - It misses spelt, seitan, semolina, malt, stock/bouillon and curry powder, and has no "may contain" concept.
  - Curated tags like "gluten-free" are hardcoded in the recipe data (`extra-pool.ts`), not computed (M16, M49).
- **Swap safety** — the curated/AI swap _is_ safety-checked. The manual replace-meal picker is not: its list is every recipe in the user's plans (`favourite-recipe.repository.ts`), and `replaceRecipe` does no safety check (M47).
- **AI provider / data disclosure** — it exists and is good: the consent sheet (per action), Profile → AI & your data, and the Privacy policy link. It is not in Preferences, where she looked first (M40).
- **Revoke / export / delete** — all three exist in Profile. The export excludes consent state, email preferences, AI logs and shopping lists (M56). No chat history is stored at all, so none is exported (good for privacy).
- **Currency** — derived from the device region at registration (`detectRegion()` in `apps/mobile/app/(auth)/register.tsx`, mapped RO→RON in `packages/utils/src/locale.ts`). The RON default is an artifact of the simulator's RO region (M11). It can be changed in Preferences → Units & currency.

**Bugs & LogBox errors**

- No red error boxes or yellow LogBox warnings appeared during the session.
- **M48 plan bleed.** Repro:
  1. On a Sunday, generate a plan for next week only.
  2. Plan → this week shows next week's meals. Shop "THIS WEEK · Week of 21 September" lists next week's items. Today shows next week's Sunday.
  - Cause: `mealPlanRepository.findActiveWithDays` returns the newest ACTIVE plan by `createdAt` and ignores `weekStartDate` (`packages/database/src/repositories/meal-plan.repository.ts` ~310). Creating a plan only archives plans for the same week. `getActive` has the same flaw.

- **M24 "Regenerate this week →"** on the post-upgrade sheet only navigates to `/meal-plan` and runs no mutation (`apps/mobile/src/features/premium/post-upgrade-sheet.tsx`).
- **M29/M50 macro change** — `withLifterProtein` is applied once `gymProfile.setupCompletedAt` is set.
- **M34 replace-list duplicates** — `buildPickerSections` removes duplicates by id only, and AI plans create new rows with the same names. The current meal is never excluded.
- **M23/M54 counters:**
  - `mealPlan.generate` reserves an AI MEAL_PLAN log for curated generation.
  - Unsaved imports count too.
  - Profile's day window uses server-local midnight while the quota uses UTC midnight.
- **M17 formatting** — "1.75" servings wraps as "1.7 / 5"; "3.1 ml cinnamon" and "2 to taste salt" appear in recipe ingredients.
- **Defaults** — `weeklyEmailReady` and `weeklyEmailRecap` default to true, and so does `autoPlanWeekly` (schema). The Sunday worker generates AI plans only if `aiDataConsentAt` is set; otherwise it generates curated plans (M38).

  - Metro "Refreshing…" banners appeared twice.

## 6. Researcher summary (out of character)

- **Privacy and consent is Chefer's standout strength for this segment.** Lena found the analytics-free experience, the per-action AI consent sheet (named processors, data list, no-training promise, equal "Not now"), one-tap revoke with scoped re-ask, instant JSON export and an itemised delete flow better than anything she uses (M03, M22, M25, M41, M43, M45). Protect this, and close the small gaps: consent history/export (M56), opt-in emails (M38), and what happens to data already sent to the AI providers (M42, M46).
- **Numbers change silently, and that is the biggest trust breaker for a data-literate user.** Finishing gym setup changed protein from 128 g to 93 g and carbs from 272 g to 307 g with no notice. The source ("1.6 g/kg because you train") exists but sits four screens deep in Preferences, and the kcal formula is shown nowhere (M09, M29, M36, M37, M50). Show a change notice and make every target tappable to its rule.
- **A real plan-selection bug shows next week's plan as "this week" in Plan, Shop and Today.** On a Sunday it produced two contradictory "today" menus. `findActiveWithDays` picks the newest ACTIVE plan and ignores `weekStartDate` (M30, M48). This will hit every user who plans ahead on a Sunday, which the product encourages ("Plan my week every Sunday").
- **Dietary safety holds for the planner, but it is implicit and has gaps that matter to a coeliac.**
  - The plan and AI swap outputs were fully vegan + GF (M12).
  - "gluten-free" tags sit on stock and curry-powder recipes with no label caveat (M16, M49).
  - The matcher misses spelt, seitan, semolina and malt (M49).
  - The manual replace picker isn't safety-filtered at all (M47).
  - No surface says "filtered for your diet", so users must probe to trust it (M33, M55).
- **Premium's first impression under-delivers.**
