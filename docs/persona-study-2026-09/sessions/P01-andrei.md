# P01 — Andrei, 24, bulking gym bro (Cluj-Napoca), power user, impatient, free-leaning

L4 · Pixel 8 emulator (Android, 1080×2400) · tier at start FREE → end FREE (never upgraded) · build f8f7f74 · 2026-09-26

## 0. Expectations before opening

- A friend said "workouts + meal plans in one app, free". Andrei expects a Strong-like logger (fast set entry, previous numbers shown, PRs) plus something that tells him what to eat to hit ~180 g protein without spending a fortune.
- He expects to sign up with one screen (ideally Google) and to be logging sets within 2 minutes.
- He'll skim everything; he won't read paragraphs.
- Deal-breakers in the first 5 minutes: a long questionnaire before seeing anything, a paywall/"start your free trial" wall, not being able to enter his own weights, or a workout logger slower than Strong.
- He is sceptical: "all-in-one apps are usually bad at both".
