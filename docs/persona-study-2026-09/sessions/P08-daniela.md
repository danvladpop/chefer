# P08 — Daniela, 45, high-school teacher in Timișoara who cooks for two and hates food waste, thrifty, free-leaning

L3 · iPhone 17 (iOS 26.5, 402×874 pt) · tier at start FREE → end PREMIUM (upgraded in S1 after hitting the second premium wall, Import Recipe; the kitchen "use it up" wall was the first) · build f8f7f74 · 2026-09-27

## 0. Expectations before opening

- She saw "zero-waste pantry" in the App Store description. Every week she does one big Carrefour shop and every week half a bag of salad, a courgette and some yoghurt end up in the bin. That bothers her more than the money.
- She expects to type in what she has (fridge + cupboard), and have the app tell her "cook this first, it goes off on Thursday".
- She saves recipes from YouTube and Facebook videos and then can never find them again; if she could paste a link and get a recipe, she'd keep the app for that alone.
- She wants to know roughly what the week's shop will cost in lei before she goes.
- Deal-breakers in the first 5 minutes: asking for her card, a diet/calorie app pretending to be a kitchen app, or having to type a lot (she types slowly).

## 6. Researcher summary (out of character)

- **The app's zero-waste promise does not exist for a free user and is invisible for a premium one.** The pantry is a Shop sub-tab that free users can't add to or remove from; the "use it up" behaviour is premium; and even on premium, mobile never shows which meals or list lines used the kitchen (web does — M55 is a platform-parity bug with outsized impact on this persona).
- **No expiry model = no answer to the persona's core question.** `pantry_items` has no date; "about to expire" can't be built without one. A per-item "use by" (even a 3-choice "today / this week / later") plus a "use soon" card on Today would convert Daniela into a daily user (tomorrow-score 2/10 today, and her stated reason is exactly this).
- **The shopping list works against waste**: 79–87 lines for 1–2 people, micro-quantities, near-duplicates, fractions, portions rounded up (2¼ → 3), and pantry items reappearing as to-buy. For a thrift persona this reads as "the app makes me buy more". Aggregating by purchasable unit and showing "you have this" would matter more than price accuracy.
- **First-run framing mismatch**: the store listing promised "zero-waste pantry", but sign-up, intents, Today and AI Chef all speak calories/goals/body; the pantry isn't mentioned until she digs. A "Use what I have" intent at onboarding that ends with "type 5 things in your fridge" would meet her in minute one.
- **Video import is the standout** (honest uncertainty flags, editable review), but it's premium and the default Link tab fails on YouTube URLs with a technical size error instead of routing to Video.
- **Trust in numbers is fragile**: week totals of 1.1k–2.9k RON and a "Saved ~389 RON" claim with no per-line explanation made her disbelieve all money figures. (Absolute prices here are mock-generated; the lack of any confidence cue or breakdown is the product issue.)
- **Re-entry has no hook**: S2 opened on calories and "next meal: breakfast" in the evening; nothing about the kitchen, tonight's dinner or the shop. No sharing with the partner, so the household loop (Radu) stays in WhatsApp.
- **Delights to keep**: leftover lunches toggle, tick-to-kitchen, natural-language "Add item", clear AI consent, big-button cook mode.
