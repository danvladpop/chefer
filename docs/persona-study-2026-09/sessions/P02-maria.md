# P02 — Maria, 30, marketing manager who lifts 3×/week and meal-preps on Sundays (Bucharest)

L2 / iPhone 17 Pro Max (iOS 26.5, dark mode) · tier at start FREE → end PREMIUM (upgraded in S1) · build f8f7f74 · 2026-09-26

## 0. Expectations before opening

- Maria tapped an Instagram ad: "your week of meals + workouts, planned". It's Saturday night, she wants Sunday's meal-prep sorted before she goes to bed.
- She expects: tell it her goal (recomp), her Mon/Wed/Fri lifting days and "no mushrooms" once, and get a week where Mon–Thu lunch is one batch-cooked dish, plus one shopping list for tomorrow's shop.
- She already pays for Strava, a period tracker and Spotify; she'd pay again _if_ this saves her the Sunday planning hour. She cancelled MyFitnessPal Premium because it was "too much logging".
- Instant-delete triggers: a 15-screen quiz before she sees anything, the app ignoring her dark mode / looking cheap, having to log every gram, or a paywall before she sees a single plan.

## 6. Researcher summary (out of character)

- **The core promise to this persona — "your week of meals + workouts, planned" — breaks at meal-prep.** There's no way to express batch cooking (same lunch Mon–Thu); "Cook once, eat twice" is dinner→next-day leftovers and is hidden in the week sheet.
