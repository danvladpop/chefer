L3 · iPhone 17 (iOS 26.5, 402×874 pt) · tier at start FREE → end PREMIUM (upgraded in S1, trigger: Household screen "your shopping list and servings are still sized for one portion") · build f8f7f74 · 2026-09-26

## 0. Expectations before opening

- A mum in the parents' WhatsApp group said "it plans the whole week's dinners and gives you the shopping list". Elena hopes it will end the nightly "what's for dinner" argument with Mihai and the kids.
- She expects to tell it once: 4 people, 2 kids, Luca is allergic to tree nuts (serious, EpiPen), Ana won't eat fish or "green stuff" — and then never see a nut recipe again.
- She expects one shopping list for the week, already sized for four, that she can share with Mihai (today they use a shared Google Keep list).
- Weeknight dinners must be ≤ 40 minutes. Romanian context: metric, RON, local supermarket ingredients.
- Deal-breakers in the first 5 minutes: any recipe with nuts after she said "nut allergy", no way to set an allergy at all, or an app that is clearly for one gym person rather than a family. She would pay if it genuinely removes the weekly planning stress.

## 4. Researcher sweep

**Where the missing / unfound things actually are**

- **Allergen reassurance on recipes** — does not exist. No allergen panel, "contains" line, "nut-free" tag or "safe for Luca" badge on recipe detail, plan cards, swap sheet or shopping list. The only place the app states it knows the allergy is inside the AI Chef reply ("Allergies (never suggest): tree nuts") and the Household/Preferences screens.
- **Is the nut allergy actually applied?** Partly. Code read (`apps/api/src/lib/curated-recipes/safety.ts`): allergies expand to a keyword list (almond, cashew, walnut, pecan, hazelnut, pistachio, macadamia, "nut", coconut, praline…) that is matched against recipe name, ingredients and steps. "Granola" is not on it, so the Greek Yogurt Parfait (40 g granola, almonds visible in the photo) is served to a tree-nut household. Discover is filtered by the merged household safety list (verified: "Almond Butter" returns nothing although such recipes exist in the DB) — but silently.
- **Ana's dislikes** — saved correctly (`household_members.dislikedIngredients = {fish, "green vegetables"}`, verified read-only in the DB), but dislikes are matched as a literal word only; "fish" doesn't expand to cod/salmon, and "green vegetables" matches nothing. Result: cod ×2 and salmon in the curated week, salmon ×2 + cod in the AI (mock) week.
- **Dinners only / fewer meals** — no setting in Preferences. ## 6. Researcher summary (out of character)

- **Allergy trust is the make-or-break, and the app never shows its safety work.** The household allergy is captured well and saved, Discover is silently filtered, but no plan card, recipe, swap list or shopping item says "checked for tree nuts". For an EpiPen-level allergy the user must see the check, not assume it (M13, M23, M29, M32, M40).
- **The safety matcher has real gaps that produced a nut-risk meal in a free plan:** granola isn't a tree-nut trigger, so a parfait with almonds in its photo was planned twice (M13, M39). Consider "may contain" ingredients (granola, chocolate, pesto, muesli, baked goods) and allergen tags on recipe data rather than keyword matching alone.
- **Dislikes don't work for category words.** Ana's "fish" and "green vegetables" were saved but ignored (cod ×2, salmon, green beans, spinach), which defeats the household promise of "less arguing at the table" (M12, M39). Expand dislikes like allergies (fish → cod/salmon…), and offer chips instead of free text.
- **The product frame is still "one person on a diet" after the user chose "Feed my household":** Today is a calorie ring, plans are 2,000 kcal/day × 3–4 meals, cook-mode ends with "log it so your nutrition stays honest", and there's no dinners-only option anywhere (M10, M11, M22, M30, M38, M41).
- **Scaling to the family is the paywall trigger, and it converts** — the Household copy ("still sized for one portion… Premium scales them for your table") was clear and honest, and she upgraded. But the Premium pitch talks about goals/nutrition, not family, and "Regenerate this week" after upgrade appeared to do nothing (M16–M20).
- **The partner is outside the product.** No list share and no household invite; today's workflow (shared Google Keep with Mihai) can't move into Chefer (M27, M48).
- **Execution delights to protect:** household onboarding presets, aisle-grouped list with one-thumb ticking and automatic pantry, two-tap swaps (manual and AI), cook mode with big controls and timers, clear AI-consent sheet (M02, M03, M21, M28, M33, M35, M37, M46).
- **Smaller defects:** egg price 568 RON (M25), "per 4 servings" nutrition label (M43), Shop opening on the current rather than planned week (M34), no-match search showing "No recipes yet" (M44), "nutty"/sesame wording unflagged in a nut-allergy household (M36).
