# P09 — Chris, 36, product designer in Leeds getting back to the gym after a new baby (2–3 short sessions/week, unpredictable times)

L1 · iPhone 16e (iOS 26, 390×844 pt) · tier at start FREE → end FREE (never upgraded: the only Premium pitch he saw — Profile — is about AI meal plans, nothing for training) · build f8f7f74 · 2026-09-27

## 0. Expectations before opening

- Chris has a 5-month-old and hasn't trained consistently for three years (was intermediate, bench 80 kg once). He gets 2–3 gym windows a week of 30–40 minutes, at times the baby decides.
- He downloaded Chefer because it was described as a plan that adapts; what he really wants is something that _adapts when he misses sessions_ instead of turning red. Strong lapsed on him because it was just a log; Headspace lapsed because of guilt-trip streak notifications.
- He expects: a short full-body routine for 2–3 days/week, no fixed weekdays, easy to log with one thumb, and a sensible response when a session is cut short.
- As a product designer he'll notice UI quality, but he's forgiving if the app respects his time.
- Deal-breakers in the first 5 minutes: a long onboarding, a streak that shames him from day one, a rigid Mon/Wed/Fri schedule he can't keep, or losing a half-logged workout.

## 4. Researcher sweep

**Where the missing / unfound things actually are**

- **Pause training** (S2 goal 2) — _exists but is unreachable._ `apps/mobile/src/features/gym/settings/settings-screen.tsx` has a "Pause training" section (1+ weeks, reason Vacation/Illness/Injury/Other, "End pause now"), backed by `gym.pause.create/end` and a `training_pauses` table; paused weeks are neutral for the streak (`packages/utils/src/gym/weeks.ts`). But the only navigation to `/gym/settings` in the whole mobile app is the offline-sync "N workouts waiting to sync / need attention" card on Gym Today (`today-screen.tsx:448`), which only renders when the outbox has items. No gear icon, no Profile/More link. For a normal online user, Pause, Reminder, Weekly goal, Units and Equipment settings cannot be reached after onboarding (M31).
- **Workout reminder** (S2 goal 4) — same screen, same problem. The reminder itself is Off/On + one hour:minute time, scheduled on _planned weekdays_ (plus a 'missed' follow-up kind in `reminders/schedule.ts`). Chris picked "No reminder" in onboarding, so he has no path back. Even if found, a fixed weekday/time model doesn't fit "2–3 sessions at unpredictable times" (M33).
- **Workout history** (S1 goal 4, S2) — exists only as the single "last session" card at the bottom of Gym Today (`gym-today-last-session`), unlabelled with an ISO date; there is no history list and Stats doesn't link to sessions (M14, M21).
- **Pause/finish-later mid-workout** (S1 goal 3) — does not exist. The logger offers Finish workout / Discard workout only (M10). Unstarted exercises aren't carried over; the next session advances to the next day (M13).
- **Missed-day handling** — by design the app never marks days red ("Never 'missed'/red (principle 3)" in `today-helpers.ts`) and offers "Skip this day", "Do another day instead" (sheet: Full Body A / B) and "Log a past workout" (backfill for the last ~2 weeks). All work, but nothing _tells_ the returning user what happened or what to do (M23, M24, M35).
- **Why bench regressed to 8 kg** (M12) — `packages/utils/src/gym/progression.ts` ~l.604–620: when fewer working sets are completed than planned, the rule is "INCOMPLETE → hold", keeping `state.next.weightKg` (the previous suggestion, 8 kg) rather than what he actually lifted (16 kg × 12 × 2). The reason copy "You skipped a set, so same targets next time" is literal, but for a user who self-corrected an under-estimated starting guess, it throws away real data.
- **Quick dinner** — no prep-time filter or "quick" tag in Discover; search matches dish/ingredient words only (M28).
- **Apple Health** — no HealthKit integration found in the mobile app (M36).

## 6. Researcher summary (out of character)

- **Gym Settings is effectively orphaned.** Pause training, Reminder, Weekly goal, Units and Equipment exist in `gym/settings` but the only link to it is the offline-sync card. Two of Chris's four S2 goals (pause, reminder) failed purely because of this missing entry point (M25, M30, M31). A gear icon on Gym Today/Routine (or a Profile section) would unlock already-built features.
- **The "life happens" moment is the product's big opportunity and currently its weakest flow.** Interrupting a session offers only Finish/Discard; unstarted exercises vanish; the next suggestion regresses the weight he actually lifted (INCOMPLETE → hold previous suggestion); and there's no "resume later" (M10, M12, M13). For parents and busy people, "save & finish later" plus carrying skipped work forward would directly serve the retention goal.
- **Session length is never asked.** Programmes are 48–50 min; the only lever is manually deleting exercises in an editor that truncates names and hides the resulting duration (M03, M05, M06). A "time available" input (onboarding + at Start) is the highest-leverage adaptation for this segment.
- **The forgiving mechanics are real but invisible.** Week-based streaks, flex weeks, pauses, backfill, skip/swap day, "Missing a session changes nothing" — all aligned with this persona — but the UI leads with "0-week streak" from minute one and a bare legend; "Skip this day" gives no feedback (M17, M24, M34). Explain and surface them; lead with the weekly ring, not the streak count.
- **Onboarding tone and logger execution are standout delights** worth protecting (M02, M09, M11, M20). Chris's SUS (65) is held up almost entirely by these.
- **Return visits have no "welcome back" or next step** — no acknowledgement of a missed day, no "still time for one this week", no history heading (M21, M23). The return screen should answer "where am I and what's the smallest thing I can do now?".
- **Gym-first users get a food-first shell elsewhere:** register copy "Meal planning…", Premium pitch only about AI meal plans, Food Today showing a generic 2,000 kcal target with no data from him, no quick-dinner filter (M01, M18, M27, M28). The one cross-link that worked was the post-workout protein nudge (M19).
- **Smaller defects:** cold rest-timer notification prompt (M08/M32), set numbering in session detail (M22), internal "research §6.1" copy and clipped chart labels in Stats (M15, M16), lateral-raise nag after a deliberate removal (M26), no Apple Health (M36).
