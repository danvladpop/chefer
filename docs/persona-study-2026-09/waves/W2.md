## 1. Goal

Rebuild onboarding and Food Today around the user's **jobs** (Train / Plan meals / Household / Use what I have /
Track), ship the job-led paywall, extend the safety filter and "Checked for" badge onto plan/Replace/Shop surfaces,

lands you on the right home screen; Today shows Tonight/Tomorrow/Shop-due cards in the right order; premium pitches
name the job it unlocks; the plan, Replace picker and shopping list all show what was checked and what's hidden.
