# Chefer: Nice to have

_Last updated 2026-10-02 · owner: Dan. Mirror of the "Chefer: Nice to have" doc. Companion to [user-needs-research-2026-10.md](./user-needs-research-2026-10.md)._

Ideas worth keeping for later, deliberately left out of the current plan while chefer consolidates. Nothing here is scheduled.

## Gym

| Idea | Why it's interesting | What it needs | Effort | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Circuits, AMRAP, EMOM and intervals with a timer** | Matches how classes are actually run (stations, rounds, work/rest); useful during a class, not only after | A block layer in the session model; a multi-phase timer with round counts and cues; old apps must not mis-show circuits | XL | Idea |
| **Trainer platform** | Trainers create routines for their clients, update their weekly meals and prepare the next sessions. One trainer brings a whole group, and trainers have asked for small-group tools for years | Talk to several trainers first. A trainer role, client links and permissions, shared routines and meal plans | XL | Needs validation |
| **Apple Health / Health Connect sync** | Watch users wouldn't type calories or runs twice; Romania is about 80% Android, so Health Connect first | Native modules and a new app build; privacy review | XL | Idea |
| **Curated combo exercises** | Classes use combos like a lunge into an overhead press; a curated list (not user-built) keeps the library governed | 20–30 combos, each with its parts' muscles and movement patterns | M | Idea |
| **Monthly benchmarks** | A repeatable test (e.g. max push-ups, a 1 km row) shows fitness improving even when workouts never repeat | A benchmark list and a simple result log | M | Idea |

## Food

| Idea | Why it's interesting | What it needs | Effort | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Menu snap** | Before ordering, snap a restaurant menu and get the best pick for what's left of your day. No checked competitor does this well | A vision AI call (premium) plus today's remaining calories and protein | M | Idea |
| **Delivery app partnerships** | Glovo, Bolt Food and Wolt are big in Romanian cities; a deep link from a suggested dish to an order closes the loop | Business talks; no code until there's a partner | XL | Idea |
| **Plan a meal out ahead** | Mark "Friday dinner: eating out" while planning; the slot stays empty, calories are kept free for it, and the shopping list doesn't buy for it | Meal slots without a recipe; today about 10 places in the code assume every slot has one. "Ate something else" covers most of the need meanwhile | L | Idea |

## Parked

Decided against for now. Keep them here so the reasoning isn't lost.

- **AI import of trainer workouts** (from a whiteboard photo or a WhatsApp message). The gym side makes no AI calls for now.
- **User-built combo exercises.** The exercise library stays governed. Curated combos above cover the need.
- **Food targets that change with any kind of training.** Class and run days appear as markers on the food plan, with no automatic calorie change.
