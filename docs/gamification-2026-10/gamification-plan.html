Chefer · gamification plan · draft 22 Oct 2026

Kitchen progress

One light reward system for the whole app. Cooking, eating, logging and training all count toward your chef rank and a weekly flame. It never rewards eating less or weighing less, and it never makes anyone feel bad for a slow week.

What goes wrong in diet apps, and how we avoid it

  • Gamifying intake can feed eating disorders. 73% of ED patients who used MyFitnessPal said it contributed, and users describe "competition to eat less each day". → No XP, badge or challenge ever scales with eating less or weighing less.
  • Broken streaks make people quit, especially when the app shames them. Khan Academy removed streaks for this reason. → Weekly unit, free freezes, pause, relight, "Best: 9 weeks".
  • Paid or transactional rewards crowd out real motivation. → XP is information about progress, not a currency. Nothing is for sale.
  • Exercise compulsion. → Rest days count, and there's no daily ring for lifting.
Every idea must pass these

Guardrails

  1. Reward behaviour, never body numbers.Logging any amount, cooking, training and PRs earn. Eating less never does.
  2. The week is the unit.Rest days are part of the plan.
  3. Forgiveness is free and automatic.Pilot lights, pause, relight.
  4. Never shame.No sad mascot, no red, no "you lost". At most one reminder a day.
  5. Compare with your past self.Social is opt-in, cooperative and effort-based.
  6. User in control.Choose your goal; celebrations Full / Subtle / Off; hide everything.
  7. Rewards explain themselves.An itemised receipt: +25 cooked at home.
  8. Cosmetic only, and free.Levels never lock a feature, and gamification is never premium.

The core loop 7

01Chef XPP1

Every meaningful action earns a few XP: logging, cooking, training, planning, shopping. XP shows progress; you can't spend it or buy it.

screen 01
02Chef ranksP1

Levels carry kitchen-brigade titles, from Kitchen Porter to Executive Chef, so a level means something. Food and gym fill the same bar, giving one level for the whole app.

screen 04
03Level-up momentP1

A full-screen celebration with the new title and what it unlocked. It shows once, can be skipped, and can be shared.

screen 04
04Kitchen receiptP1

Every reward lists why it was earned ("+25 cooked at home, +10 first time"). Feedback that explains itself keeps motivation intrinsic.

screen 02screen 03
05Daily soft capP1

Past about 150 XP a day, actions still count but earn less. Logging 20 snacks shouldn't beat cooking one dinner.

06Endowed startP1

Finishing onboarding drops you 20% into level 1 with a "Welcome to the kitchen" badge. A pre-filled bar gets finished more often (34% vs 19%).

07Back-dated XPP1

On launch, existing users get XP and badges for the history they already have (workouts, PRs, cooked meals). Day one then feels like a reward, not a reset.

Streaks & consistency 7

08The Flame (weekly streak)P1

It grows by one each week you reach your active-day goal (default 3 of 7, adjustable). Rest days never break it.

screen 05
09Active day = any real actionP1

Cooking, logging one meal or training makes the day active. A small qualifying action is what made Duolingo's streak stick.

10Pilot lights (freezes)P1

You earn one every 4 good weeks (max 2, or 3 from Chef de Partie), and it's used automatically on a short week. This extends the gym "flex tokens" that already exist.

screen 05
11Pause my flameP1

Holiday or illness mode freezes the streak for up to 4 weeks. It works like Apple's ring pause in watchOS 11.

screen 12
12RelightP2

If the flame goes out, one extra active day within 7 days brings it back. After that you see "Best: 9 weeks" and a fresh start, never a loss.

13Gym streak becomes a sub-flameP1

The existing weekly training-goal streak stays as it is and feeds the main Flame. Nothing users already have is taken away.

14Flame milestonesP1

Badges at 4, 8, 12, 26 and 52 weeks; the crown at a year. Each milestone gets a tier-3 celebration.

15Today's threeP1

Three personalised mini-quests a day, taken from your plan: log breakfast, cook dinner, hit protein. Finishing all three gives a bonus.

screen 01

Three challenges each week (try a new recipe, green week, leg-day hero), and any two count. Having the choice keeps it light.

18Monday fresh startP2

Challenges and boards reset on Monday with a "new week" card. This uses the fresh-start effect instead of dwelling on last week.

An opt-in Sunday pledge such as "I'll cook 4 dinners", for bonus XP if you do it. Duolingo's version raised D7 by 14%; ours costs nothing if you miss.

Eating & logging 3

28Honest loggingP1

Logging always earns the same XP, including on days over target. The app never rewards eating less.

29Full day loggedP1

Logging every planned meal gives a small bonus on top of the existing "day complete" confetti. It is consistency, not perfection.

30Good-food goalsP1

Protein reached, or a full serving of veg, count as positive behaviours. There is never an "under calories" reward.

Gym 5

31Live PR bannerP1

The moment you tick a set that beats your best, a banner fires mid-workout; the summary then celebrates it in full (📱03). It builds on the existing detectPrs.

32PR wallP2

A per-lift timeline of PRs with the climb chart. It shows "you, 8 weeks ago" next to "you, today".

Badges for gym-culture firsts: the 1-plate bench (60 kg), the 2-plate squat (100 kg), benching your bodyweight. They're relative to lifting history, never to body weight goals.

Controls & safety 4

56Celebration levelP1

Full party, Subtle or Off; Off still records everything. Respects reduced motion (MO-07 already does).

screen 12
57Hide it allP1

One toggle hides XP, levels and the flame strip, as Headspace lets you hide the streak. It works together with the existing "protein-only" numbers mode.

screen 12
58Gentle remindersP1

At most one a day, only if you haven't opened Chefer, with automatic back-off after 3 ignored reminders. Copy is invitational and lint-checked against guilt words.

No XP, badge or challenge may scale with eating less or weighing less, and this is enforced in code review and tests. If logs show very low intake for days, food rewards go quiet and the app shows support info instead.

A typical engaged week earns ≈600 XP. Going from level n to n+1 takes 80 + 60·n XP, so level 8 comes in about a month and level 20 in about 5 months.

Gaps to close

  • A finished cook isn't stored on the server; today it is only an analytics event
  • No generic event log → add an append-only ProgressEvent ledger with idempotency keys
  • Shared rules as pure functions in @chefer/utils; new progress.* procedures are additive, so shipped binaries are safe
  • New gamification feature flag; web shows the same strip and badges (parity rule)
Ship the low-risk core first

Rollout

P1 · Core
≈2–3 weeks. XP, ranks, level-up, receipts, the Flame, pilot lights, pause, Today's three, cook and PR rewards, honest logging, 25 tiered achievements, back-dated XP, Biscuit cameo, weekly recap, controls. Progress bars and goals are what nutrition users value most, and they carry the least risk.
P2 · Delight
Tip jar, weekly specials, monthly events, relight, signature dish, passport, PR wall, Biscuit wardrobe (only if the dogfood says yes), cosmetics, cheers, share cards, Chefer Wrapped.
P3 · Together
Duo flame, crew challenges, household co-op, trainer kudos, seasonal bingo, on this day. Social carries the most risk, so it waits for P1 data.

Measure: D7/D30 retention, goal-met weeks per user, cook-mode completions, the share of users who turn celebrations off (a pressure signal), and reminder opt-outs.
