# Tester feedback — 2026-10-02

The source is the owner's Google Doc ("tab t.0"). There were two inputs:

- the interview guide from `docs/product/user-needs-research-2026-10.md`, answered by one class-goer (the owner's wife,
  "Tester W");
- two improvements she noted after using the app that day.

The trainer questions were **not answered**. This is n = 1, so treat it as a signal, not validation.

## Interview answers (condensed)

1. **Last class.** It was leg day: leg press, barbell deadlifts, barbell reverse lunges, lateral duck-unders with
   dumbbells, box jumps, planks. Her week is 2 leg days, 1 upper-body day and sometimes 1 cardio day.
2. **What to do next.** The trainer tells her after each exercise.
3. **Exercises.** Mostly standard gym exercises. Sometimes the trainer invents combos (e.g. jump squats combined with
   jump lunges) "which cannot be added as regular exercises".
4. **Progress.** She checks calories burned after each gym day, weighs herself, and looks at muscle growth.
5. **Watch.** She wears a gym watch and uses **Google Health**. Each week she checks calories burned, cardio done and
   whether she reached her goals.
6. **Why she goes back.** She feels better, and her back doesn't hurt (it hurts when she skips). She also likes the
   community and her friends in class.
7. **Food.** No difference between gym and non-gym days. They cook healthy and sometimes order in. She **doesn't count
   calories**; she tries not to overeat and to eat **more protein** than carbs or fat.
8. **Wanted from an app.** "As simple as possible." She wants to see gym progress: **sessions done this week**, the
   **calories reached**, and optionally the exercises, "or at least … what kind of training it was: cardio, legs, upper
   body, abs".

## Improvements needed

1. **Text and controls are too small.** Her trainer couldn't read the text without glasses. Larger fonts would help.
2. **Freestyle swap asks an unneeded question.** When building a freestyle workout and swapping a wrongly picked exercise,
   a popup asks whether to change it for today or for the routine. A freestyle workout has no routine, so the question
   isn't needed.
