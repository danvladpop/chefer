Grandma's Lentil Soup

A one-pot red lentil soup that costs almost nothing and feeds four. Serves 4.

What you need

  • 300 g red lentils
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 1.5 l vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp cumin

How to make it

Dice the onion and carrots and soften them in a splash of oil. Add the cumin and toast for a minute. Tip in the lentils and stock, then simmer 25 minutes until the lentils collapse. Blend half the pot for a creamy-but-textured finish. Season well.

Roughly 320 kcal per bowl.
