Best Ever Peanut Chicken Satay

My family begs for this every single week. We first tried satay on our trip to Bangkok in 2019 — the smell of the street grills, the sticky heat, the little plastic stools… I have chased that flavour ever since, and after 47 test batches I can finally say: this is it.

The secret is using real peanut butter & full-fat coconut milk.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast
  • 120 g peanut butter
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic

Method

  1. Slice the chicken into strips and marinate 30 minutes.
  2. Whisk peanut butter, coconut milk, soy sauce and sugar into a sauce.
  3. Grill the skewers 8–10 minutes, turning once. Serve with the sauce.
