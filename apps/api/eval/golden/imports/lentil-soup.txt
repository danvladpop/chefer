Weeknight Red Lentil Soup

Serves 4. About 320 kcal per serving.

Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 250 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 x 400 g can chopped tomatoes
- 1.2 litres vegetable stock
- juice of half a lemon
- salt and pepper

Method
1. Warm the oil in a large pot and soften the onion and carrot for 8 minutes.
2. Stir in the garlic and cumin and cook for one minute.
3. Add the lentils, tomatoes and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils collapse.
4. Blend half the soup, stir back in, season and finish with lemon juice.
