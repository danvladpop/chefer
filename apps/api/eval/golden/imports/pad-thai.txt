Quick Prawn Pad Thai

Ingredients (serves 2)
150 g flat rice noodles
200 g raw king prawns
2 eggs
2 tbsp fish sauce
1 tbsp tamarind paste
1 tbsp brown sugar
2 spring onions, sliced
100 g bean sprouts
2 tbsp roasted peanuts, crushed
1 tbsp vegetable oil
1 lime

Soak the noodles in hot water for 8 minutes and drain. Mix fish sauce, tamarind and sugar. Heat the oil in a wok, stir-fry the prawns until pink, push aside and scramble the eggs. Add the noodles and sauce and toss for 2 minutes, then add bean sprouts and spring onion. Serve topped with peanuts and lime wedges.

Per serving: 610 kcal.
