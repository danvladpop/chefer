Lemon & Herb Chicken Traybake — a one-pan dinner for two

Prep 10 min · Cook 35 min · Serves 2

You'll need: 2 chicken breasts (about 350g total), 400g baby potatoes halved, 1 red pepper cut into strips, 1 red onion in wedges, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 lemon (zest and juice), 1 tsp dried oregano, a few sprigs of thyme.

Heat the oven to 200C. Toss the potatoes, pepper and onion with half the oil and roast for 15 minutes. Rub the chicken with the rest of the oil, lemon zest, oregano and thyme, nestle it among the vegetables and roast for 20 minutes more until cooked through. Squeeze over the lemon juice before serving.

Nutrition per serving: 540 kcal, 46 g protein, 45 g carbs, 18 g fat.
