Three-Bean Vegan Chili (batch cook, 6 portions)

olive oil 2 tbsp · onions 2 · garlic 4 cloves · red peppers 2 · chili powder 2 tsp · ground cumin 2 tsp · smoked paprika 1 tsp · kidney beans 2 cans (drained) · black beans 1 can (drained) · chickpeas 1 can (drained) · chopped tomatoes 2 cans · tomato purée 2 tbsp · vegetable stock 300 ml · dark chocolate 10 g

Soften the onions and peppers in the oil, add garlic and spices for a minute, then everything else except the chocolate. Simmer 40 minutes, stir in the chocolate at the end. Freezes well.
Roughly 330 kcal a portion.
