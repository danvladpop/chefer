Gran's banana bread (copied from the card, some of it smudged)

3 very ripe bananas mashed
75 g melted butter
150 g sugar (she wrote "a bit less is fine")
1 egg beaten
1 tsp vanilla
1 tsp baking soda
pinch salt
190 g plain flour

Mix butter into bananas. Mix in sugar, egg, vanilla. Sprinkle soda + salt over, then flour. Pour into loaf tin. Bake 175C about 1 hour. Makes 10 slices.
